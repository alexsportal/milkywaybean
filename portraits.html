Terms of Service

Payment

  • A non-refundable deposit of 50% of the total commission price is required before the Artist begins any work. 
  • The remaining 50% balance is due upon completion, before the final artwork or digital file is delivered to the Client. 
  • Accepted payment methods: Zelle, Venmo, or PayPal (Goods & Services). 
  • No checks or money orders will be accepted under any circumstances. 
  • Failure to pay the remaining balance within 7 days of completion will result in forfeiture of the artwork. The deposit will not be refunded. 

Reference Photos

  • The Client is responsible for providing clear, high-quality reference photos. 
  • Photos should be well-lit and show the subject(s) clearly. Poor-quality references may affect the final result. 
  • The Artist reserves the right to request additional or better reference photos before beginning. 
  • The Client warrants that they have the right to use any photos submitted as references. of completion will result in forfeiture of the artwork. The deposit will not be refunded. 

Revisions & Approval

  • The Artist will provide up to two (2) rounds of minor revisions at no additional cost (e.g., color adjustments, minor detail changes). 
  • Major revisions (e.g., changing the composition, adding/removing subjects, or significant rework) may incur an additional fee, to be agreed upon in writing before work begins. 
  • The Client will be shown a work-in-progress (WIP) image at roughly 50% completion for feedback. 
  • Once the final artwork is approved and delivered, no further revisions are included. 

Cancellations

  • The 50% deposit is strictly non-refundable under all circumstances, as it compensates the Artist for time and materials already committed.
  • If the Client cancels after work has begun but before completion, the deposit is forfeited and no refund will be issued.
  • If the Artist is unable to complete the commission for any reason, the full deposit will be refunded to the Client.

Delivery

  • Oil Paintings: Shipped via a tracked and insured carrier, or available for local pickup. Shipping costs are the responsibility of the Client and will be added to the final invoice.
  • Digital Portraits: Delivered as a high-resolution digital file (PNG or JPEG) via email or a file-sharing link within 48 hours of final payment.
  • The Artist is not responsible for damage that occurs during shipping once the package has been handed to the carrier.

Copyright & Usage

  • The Client receives a personal, non-commercial license to display and enjoy the artwork.
  • The Artist reserves the right to photograph and share the completed artwork in their portfolio, website, and social media, unless the Client requests confidentiality in writing before work begins.
  • The Client may not reproduce, sell, or use the artwork for commercial purposes without the Artist's written permission.
